A SUPPORTIVE SPACE TO START, LEARN AND GROW.

i+1 is a microlearning platform designed to support women learning computing through short courses, shared knowledge and meaningful community connections.

The name is inspired by the idea of i = i + 1 — making progress one small step at a time. Learn from each other!
